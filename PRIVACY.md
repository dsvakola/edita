# Edita Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026
**Applies to:** Edita 1.3.0 and later, for Windows (including copies installed from the Microsoft Store)

Edita is a text and code editor developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar & Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar, for Vidyasagar Robotics Academy.

## Summary

Edita does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. Edita itself makes no network connections.

## What Edita stores on your PC

Edita keeps the following only on your own computer, in your Windows user profile:

| What | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your preferences (theme, font, font size, indent, editor colours, editing helpers) | `%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.edita\settings.json` | To remember your settings |
| The files open when Edita was last closed (paths only) | `%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.edita\session.json` | To reopen your tabs next time |
| The last 10 files you opened or saved (paths only) | `%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.edita\recent.json` | For File › Open Recent |
| Web view cache used to show Edita's window | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.edita\` | Needed by Microsoft Edge WebView2 |

Edita reads and writes your documents only when you open or save them. The contents of your documents are never stored anywhere else and never sent anywhere.

If you use **Tools › Make Edita the Default Text Editor**, Edita adds entries under `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Classes` and `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Edita` in the Windows registry so that Windows can offer Edita for text and code files. Uninstalling Edita removes them.

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling Edita and deleting the two folders above.

## Network use

Edita does not connect to the internet. The only times anything outside Edita is opened are when **you** choose to:

- click a web link in Help or About (for example https://vsa.edu.in/software/ or this policy) — it opens in your web browser;
- click the e-mail address — it opens your e-mail app.

What happens then is covered by the privacy policy of that website, browser or e-mail service.

The Edita installer may download Microsoft Edge WebView2 from Microsoft if it is not already on your PC (it is part of Windows 10 and 11). Microsoft Edge WebView2 is a Windows component; any diagnostic data it sends is governed by your Windows privacy settings and the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), not by Edita.

## Children

Edita is suitable for students of all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.
